# Specials

## Baileys
A cozy, indulgent drink perfect for colder nights. The smooth Baileys blends seamlessly with hot chocolate, making it the ultimate comforting cocktail. Can be done in cold as well, or with fresh milk.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Baileys
- 200ml Hot Chocolate/ Milk
- Whipped Cream (optional)


**Instructions:**  
1. Prepare hot chocolate/ fresh milk.
2. Stir in Baileys.
3. Top with whipped cream if desired.
4. Add ice (optional) if prefer served cold.
