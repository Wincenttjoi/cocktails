# Specials

## Mai Tai (No Orgeat Syrup)  
A tropical cocktail that balances the richness of rum with a refreshing citrus twist. The honey adds a touch of sweetness and gives the drink a smooth finish.

**Ingredients:**  
- 30ml Dark Rum
- 30ml White Rum
- 15ml Cointreau  
- 15ml Lime Juice  
- 10ml Honey  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a shaker, combine white rum, dark rum, Cointreau, lime juice, and honey.  
2. Add ice and shake well.  
3. Strain into a glass filled with crushed ice.

## Baileys & Milk
A cozy, indulgent drink perfect for colder nights. The smooth Baileys blends seamlessly with hot chocolate, making it the ultimate comforting cocktail. Can be done in cold as well, or with fresh milk.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Baileys
- 200ml Hot Chocolate/ Milk
- Whipped Cream (optional)


**Instructions:**  
1. Prepare hot chocolate/ fresh milk.
2. Stir in Baileys.
3. Top with whipped cream if desired.
4. Add ice (optional) if prefer served cold.

**Profile:**  
- ☕ Warmth: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐

## Choya on the Rocks  
A smooth and refreshing drink that allows the natural flavors of Choya to shine. The ice slightly dilutes the sweetness, making it even more enjoyable.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Choya  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Pour Choya over the ice.  
3. Stir gently and enjoy!

## Cointreau Fizz  
A refreshing and light drink that pairs the sweetness of Cointreau with the zing of lime juice and the effervescence of soda water. Perfect for a casual and refreshing drink.  

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Cointreau  
- 25ml Lime Juice  
- Soda Water  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add Cointreau and lime juice to a glass filled with ice.  
2. Top with soda water and stir gently.  
3. Garnish with a lime wheel.
