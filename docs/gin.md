# Gin Cocktails

## Gin & Tonic
A refreshing and classic gin cocktail.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- 150ml Soda Water  
- Lime Wedge  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle a sugar cube with a squeezed lime wedge at the bottom of a glass.  
2. Fill with ice, pour in gin, and top with soda water.  
3. Stir gently.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐

## Classic Gin Old Fashioned
A spirit-forward take on the Old Fashioned using gin.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters (optional)  
- Lemon or Orange Peel  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with a few drops of water (and bitters if using) in a glass.  
2. Add ice and gin, then stir until well chilled.  
3. Garnish with a twist of lemon or orange peel.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐  
- 🍂 Bitterness: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐

## Gin Fizz
A light and fizzy gin cocktail with a citrusy touch.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- 25ml Lime Juice  
- 100ml Soda Water  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with lime juice in a shaker.  
2. Add gin and ice, shake well.  
3. Strain into a glass and top with soda water.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Green Tea Gin Cooler
A refreshing tea-based cocktail with herbal notes.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 100ml Green Tea (chilled)  
- 10ml Simple Syrup  
- Lemon Wedge  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Brew green tea and let it chill.  
2. In a glass, combine gin, green tea, and simple syrup over ice.  
3. Stir and garnish with a lemon wedge.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐  
- 🍂 Bitterness: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐
