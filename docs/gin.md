# Gin Cocktails

## White Lady  
A refreshing and citrusy cocktail with a hint of sophistication. The gin pairs wonderfully with the Cointreau, while the lemon juice adds a zesty brightness that makes this drink light yet flavorful.

**Ingredients:**  
- 40ml Gin  
- 20ml Cointreau  
- 20ml Fresh Lemon Juice  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a cocktail shaker, combine gin, Cointreau, and fresh lemon juice.  
2. Add ice and shake vigorously.  
3. Strain into a chilled glass.

## Pink Gin  
A simple drink where Angostura bitters are the star, adding complexity to the gin.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 4 dashes Angostura Bitters  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Pour the gin and bitters into a mixing glass with ice.  
2. Stir well, then strain into a chilled martini or coupe glass.

## Pegu Club  
A strong and aromatic cocktail with a citrusy kick and a slightly bitter finish, perfect for those who enjoy a well-balanced, sophisticated drink.  

**Ingredients:**  
- 45ml Gin  
- 15ml Cointreau  
- 15ml Lime Juice  
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add gin, Cointreau, lime juice, and bitters to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake well and strain into a chilled cocktail glass.  
3. Garnish with a lime wheel.  

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Gin & Tonic  
The ultimate refreshing classic! A crisp and clean drink that’s as simple as it is satisfying. Perfect for those who love a little fizz with their gin. This cocktail has been a favorite for a reason—it’s light, sharp, and effortlessly easy to sip on.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- 150ml Tonic Water
- Lime Wedge  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle a sugar cube with a squeezed lime wedge at the bottom of a glass.  
2. Fill with ice, pour in gin, and top with soda water.  
3. Stir gently to combine all the flavors.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐

## Gin Old Fashioned  
A twist on the traditional Old Fashioned, but with a gin spin! Strong, aromatic, and packed with bold flavors, this cocktail is for those who appreciate a spirit-forward drink that makes a statement. You might find yourself saying, "I’ll have another."

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters
- Lemon or Orange Peel  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with a few drops of water (and bitters if using) in a glass.  
2. Add ice and gin, then stir until well chilled.  
3. Garnish with a twist of lemon or orange peel for that perfect citrusy aroma.

- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐  
- 🍂 Bitterness: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Green Tea Gin Cooler  
A refreshing twist on the classic gin cocktail, this drink combines herbal green tea with the crispness of gin. It's perfect if you want something light, soothing, and unique. If you’re a tea lover, this one's for you!

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Gin  
- 100ml Green Tea (chilled)  
- 10ml Simple Syrup  
- Lemon Wedge  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Brew your green tea and let it chill.  
2. In a glass, combine gin, chilled green tea, and simple syrup over ice.  
3. Stir well and garnish with a lemon wedge for a citrusy finish.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐  
- 🍂 Bitterness: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐

## Oolong Gin Twist  
For those who enjoy a bit of sophistication in their cocktails, this oolong tea-infused gin drink brings together floral, earthy flavors with the crispness of gin. It’s a complex, aromatic drink that’s perfect for sipping slowly and savoring every drop.

- 50ml Gin

- Lime Wedge  
- Ice
