# Darkrum Cocktails

## Dark & Stormy  
A bold and spicy cocktail that’s like a little storm in your glass—refreshing, spicy, and a kick of lime to balance it all out. It's a great drink if you want something with a bit of personality, but not too complicated. The dark rum gives it depth, while the lime adds that zesty zing to keep it interesting.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 150ml Soda Water  
- Lime Wedge  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Pour in the dark rum and top it off with soda water.  
3. Squeeze in a lime wedge, give it a little stir, and voilà!

## Dark Rum Honey Lemonade  
A smooth and sweet drink with honey and a light touch of citrus. It's like a hug in a glass with the perfect balance of warmth and zest. The dark rum pairs wonderfully with honey's richness and lemon's tang, creating a comforting, flavorful cocktail.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 15ml Honey  
- 25ml Lemon Juice  
- 100ml Soda Water  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a glass, combine dark rum and honey.  
2. Add lemon juice, soda water, and ice.  
3. Stir well to combine all the flavors.

- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐

## Dark Rum Milk Punch  
A creamy, comforting drink with dark rum. Perfect if you’re in the mood for something indulgent and smooth, like a dessert in a glass. The creamy texture, combined with dark rum's bold flavors, makes this cocktail feel like a special treat.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 100ml Milk  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add the sugar cube to a glass with a little milk to dissolve it.  
2. Add dark rum, the rest of the milk, and ice.  
3. Stir gently and serve.

**Profile:**  
- 🥛 Creaminess: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐

## Dark Rum Sour  
A zesty, citrusy dark rum drink with a touch of sweetness. Perfect for those who want a refreshing and tangy drink with a little bit of a bite! The lime gives it brightness, while the dark rum adds depth and complexity.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 25ml Lime Juice  
- 15ml Simple Syrup  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a shaker, combine dark rum, lime juice, and simple syrup.  
2. Shake well and strain into a glass with ice.

- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐
