# Dark Rum Cocktails

## Dark & Stormy
A bold and spicy cocktail that's both refreshing and warming.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 150ml Soda Water  
- Lime Wedge  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Pour in dark rum and top with soda water.  
3. Squeeze a lime wedge and stir.

**Profile:**

## Dark Rum Honey Lemonade
A smooth and sweet drink with honey and a light touch of citrus.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 15ml Honey  
- 25ml Lemon Juice  
- 100ml Soda Water  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a glass, combine dark rum and honey.  
2. Add lemon juice, soda water, and ice.  
3. Stir well.

**Profile:**

## Dark Rum Milk Punch
A creamy, comforting drink with dark rum.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 100ml Milk  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add the sugar cube to a glass with a little milk to dissolve it.  
2. Add dark rum, the rest of the milk, and ice.  
3. Stir gently and serve.

**Profile:**  
- 🥛 Creaminess: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Dark Rum Sour
A zesty, citrusy dark rum drink with a touch of sweetness.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Dark Rum  
- 25ml Lime Juice  
- 15ml Simple Syrup  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a shaker, combine dark rum, lime juice, and simple syrup.  
2. Shake well and strain into a glass with ice.
