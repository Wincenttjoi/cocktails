# Bourbon & Japanese Whiskey Cocktails

## Bourbon & Coke / Sprite
A straightforward and popular drink with whiskey, offering a smooth and mellow flavor profile. The bourbon adds a rich depth while the Coke gives it that familiar sweetness. It’s an easy, no-fuss drink that’s perfect for any occasion.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 150ml Coke / Sprite
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Pour in bourbon whiskey and top with Coke / Sprite.  
3. Stir gently.

## Whiskey Sour  
A classic and tangy cocktail with bourbon or Japanese whiskey. The balance of the sharp lemon and smooth whiskey makes this drink both refreshing and satisfying, perfect for those who enjoy a bit of sourness in their drinks.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 25ml Lemon Juice  
- 15ml Simple Syrup
- egg white
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a shaker, combine bourbon whiskey, lemon juice, and simple syrup.  
2. Shake well and strain into a glass with ice.

## Bourbon Honey Lemonade  
A smooth and slightly sweet cocktail with honey and citrus. The honey gives it a comforting, rich sweetness, while the lemon juice adds a refreshing tang. A great choice for those who like a softer, sweeter drink with a hint of zest.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 15ml Honey  
- 25ml Lemon Juice  
- 100ml Soda Water  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a glass, combine bourbon whiskey and honey.  
2. Add lemon juice, soda water, and ice.  
3. Stir well.

## Bourbon Milk Punch  
A rich, creamy drink with bourbon whiskey. The combination of bourbon and milk creates a velvety texture, while the sugar cube adds just the right amount of sweetness. Perfect for those seeking a comforting, dessert-like cocktail.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 100ml Milk  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add the sugar cube to a glass with a little milk to dissolve it.  
2. Add bourbon whiskey, the rest of the milk, and ice.  
3. Stir gently and serve.

**Profile:**  
- 🥛 Creaminess: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
