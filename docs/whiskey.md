# Whiskey Cocktails

## Old Fashioned  
A timeless cocktail that lets the bitters shine with a simple, balanced mix.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon or Rye Whiskey  
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- Lemon or Orange Peel
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Muddle the sugar cube with bitters and a splash of water in a rocks glass.  
2. Add ice, then pour in the whiskey.  
3. Stir and garnish with a twist of orange peel.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Gold Rush  
A simple, refreshing whiskey cocktail with honey and lemon, balanced with a dash of bitters.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon  
- 20ml Fresh Lemon Juice  
- 15ml Honey Syrup (1:1 honey and water)  
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Combine bourbon, lemon juice, honey syrup, and bitters in a shaker.  
2. Add ice and shake well.  
3. Strain into a rocks glass with ice.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Manhattan  
A classic cocktail with a deep flavor profile, the bitters complement the whiskey and vermouth.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon / Rye Whiskey  
- 25ml Sweet Vermouth  
- 2 dashes Angostura Bitters  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add whiskey, vermouth, and bitters into a mixing glass with ice.  
2. Stir until chilled, then strain into a chilled coupe or martini glass.  
3. Garnish with a cherry or a twist of lemon peel.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Whiskey Sour  
A classic and tangy cocktail with bourbon or Japanese whiskey. The balance of the sharp lemon and smooth whiskey makes this drink both refreshing and satisfying, perfect for those who enjoy a bit of sourness in their drinks.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon / Japanese whiskey
- 25ml Lemon Juice  
- 15ml Simple Syrup
- 1 dash Angostura Bitters  
- 1 Egg White
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a shaker, combine bourbon whiskey, lemon juice, and simple syrup.  
2. Shake well and strain into a glass with ice.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Bourbon Milk Punch  
A rich, creamy drink with bourbon whiskey. The combination of bourbon and milk creates a velvety texture, while the sugar cube adds just the right amount of sweetness. Perfect for those seeking a comforting, dessert-like cocktail.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 100ml Milk  
- 15ml Honey
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add the sugar cube to a glass with a little milk to dissolve it.  
2. Add bourbon whiskey, the rest of the milk, and ice.  
3. Stir gently and serve.

**Profile:**  
- 🥛 Creaminess: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Japanese Whiskey Highball  
A simple and refreshing cocktail with Japanese whiskey, making for a crisp, light drink. The soda water keeps it bubbly and refreshing, while the whiskey’s smoothness is showcased perfectly in this minimalist concoction.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Japanese Whiskey  
- 150ml Soda Water  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.
