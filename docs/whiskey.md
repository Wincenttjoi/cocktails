# Bourbon & Japanese Whiskey Cocktails

## Bourbon & Coke
A straightforward and popular drink with whiskey.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 150ml Coke  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Pour in bourbon whiskey and top with Coke.  
3. Stir gently.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Whiskey Sour
A classic and tangy cocktail with bourbon or Japanese whiskey.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 25ml Lemon Juice  
- 15ml Simple Syrup  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a shaker, combine bourbon whiskey, lemon juice, and simple syrup.  
2. Shake well and strain into a glass with ice.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Bourbon Honey Lemonade
A smooth and slightly sweet cocktail with honey and citrus.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 15ml Honey  
- 25ml Lemon Juice  
- 100ml Soda Water  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a glass, combine bourbon whiskey and honey.  
2. Add lemon juice, soda water, and ice.  
3. Stir well.

- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

## Bourbon Milk Punch
A rich, creamy drink with bourbon whiskey.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml Bourbon Whiskey  
- 100ml Milk  
- 1 Sugar Cube  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. Add the sugar cube to a glass with a little milk to dissolve it.  
2. Add bourbon whiskey, the rest of the milk, and ice.  
3. Stir gently and serve.

**Profile:**  
- 🥛 Creaminess: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐⭐
