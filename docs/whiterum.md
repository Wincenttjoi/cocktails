# White Rum Cocktails

## Mai Tai (No Orgeat Syrup)  
A tropical cocktail that balances the richness of rum with a refreshing citrus twist. The honey adds a touch of sweetness and gives the drink a smooth finish.

**Ingredients:**  
- 30ml Dark Rum
- 30ml White Rum
- 15ml Cointreau  
- 15ml Lime Juice  
- 10ml Honey  
- Ice  

**Instructions:**  
1. In a shaker, combine white rum, dark rum, Cointreau, lime juice, and honey.  
2. Add ice and shake well.  
3. Strain into a glass filled with crushed ice.

## Rum & Coke  
A classic and simple cocktail that's always a hit. The combination of white rum and Coke is a crowd-pleaser, offering a smooth and slightly sweet drink with a touch of rum warmth.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml White Rum  
- Coke (fill)  
- Lime Wedge (optional)

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Pour in white rum and top with Coke.  
3. Garnish with a lime wedge, if desired.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐⭐

## Daiquiri  
A tart and refreshing rum-based cocktail. The fresh lime juice balances the sweetness from the sugar syrup, making it a crisp and vibrant drink.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml White Rum  
- 25ml Fresh Lime Juice (or Lemon)  
- 15ml Sugar Syrup

**Instructions:**  
1. Add white rum, lime juice, and sugar syrup to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake well and strain into a chilled glass.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐

## Rum Lemonade  
A light and refreshing cocktail perfect for warm days. The combination of white rum and lemonade is simple but satisfying, offering a bright and crisp flavor.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml White Rum  
- 100ml Lemonade (or lemon juice, water, sugar mix)

**Instructions:**  
1. Pour white rum into a glass with ice.  
2. Top with lemonade and stir gently.
