# White Rum Cocktails

## Rum & Coke
A classic and simple cocktail that's always a hit.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml White Rum  
- Coke (fill)  
- Lime Wedge (optional)

**Instructions:**  
1. Fill a glass with ice.  
2. Pour in white rum and top with Coke.  
3. Garnish with a lime wedge, if desired.

## Daiquiri
A tart and refreshing rum-based cocktail.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml White Rum  
- 25ml Fresh Lime Juice (or Lemon)  
- 15ml Sugar Syrup

**Instructions:**  
1. Add white rum, lime juice, and sugar syrup to a shaker with ice.  
2. Shake well and strain into a chilled glass.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🔥 Strength: ⭐⭐⭐  
- 🍬 Sweetness: ⭐⭐

## Rum Sour
A smooth and tangy drink with a rum base.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml White Rum  
- 25ml Fresh Lemon Juice  
- 15ml Sugar Syrup

**Instructions:**  
1. Shake white rum, lemon juice, and sugar syrup with ice.  
2. Strain into a chilled glass and serve.

**Profile:**  
- 🍋 Acidity: ⭐⭐⭐

## Rum Lemonade
A light and refreshing cocktail perfect for warm days.

**Ingredients:**  
- 50ml White Rum  
- 100ml Lemonade (or lemon juice, water, sugar mix)

**Instructions:**  
1. Pour white rum into a glass with ice.  
2. Top with lemonade and stir gently.
